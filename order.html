Ordering FAQ

Know what happens next.

Can I use my business logo?

Yes. Upload the clearest logo file you have. High-contrast artwork usually produces the cleanest engraved patch.

Can I order patches without hats?

Yes. Choose Patch Only in the builder. Standalone custom patches start at $5 each.

How does the bulk bonus work?

For every 12 hats purchased, you receive 1 additional hat free. Twelve purchased hats means thirteen hats received.

Are all colors always in stock?

The builder shows verified model-specific catalog colors. Some may require special ordering, so immediate blank-hat availability is confirmed before production.
