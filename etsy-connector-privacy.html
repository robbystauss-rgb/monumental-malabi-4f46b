REC Mama Made Etsy Connector Privacy

This private connector is used by the owner of REC Mama Made to manage that owner's Etsy shop. It is not a public multi-seller service.

Data handled

The connector may retrieve and update Etsy shop and listing data that the authorized shop owner requests, including listing titles, descriptions, tags, prices, quantities, personalization settings, shop text, and listing images.

Authentication

Etsy OAuth tokens and connector credentials are stored in Netlify-hosted environment variables or Netlify storage and are not intentionally exposed in public source code.

Sharing

The connector does not sell Etsy account information or intentionally share it with advertisers. Data is sent only to services required to perform the requested Etsy management workflow.

Deletion

The connector does not expose an Etsy listing-deletion action.

Contact

For questions, contact REC Mama Made through its Etsy shop messaging channel.
